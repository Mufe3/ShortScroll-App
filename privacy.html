Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Version 1.0.0

1. Data Collection & Processing

ShortScroll is designed from the ground up with Privacy by Default. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or device IDs.
  • No Browsing History: We do not track, record, or monitor your web browsing activity.
  • No Account Access: We do not read, intercept, or access your social media accounts, login credentials, cookies, or private direct messages.

2. Permissions & Why They Are Needed

ShortScroll requests strictly the bare minimum permissions necessary to function:

Host Permissions (*.tiktok.com, *.instagram.com, *.facebook.com, *.youtube.com):

Required exclusively to inject the auto-scroll script on supported short-video feeds. The script detects video completion events and simulates arrow-key down events to advance the feed.

ActiveTab & Scripting:

Used solely to check if your active tab is currently on a supported video feed so you can toggle auto-scroll on or off via the popup.

Local Storage:

Used exclusively on your local device to remember your interface preferences (such as playback speed and loop count). No data ever leaves your computer.

3. Third-Party Services & Telemetry

ShortScroll contains zero third-party tracking scripts, zero telemetry, and zero advertisements. All computations and video state checks occur locally in your browser memory or inside the local desktop WebView engine.

4. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions, concerns, or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the official GitHub repository.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Hands-Free Auto-Advance:** Intelligently detects video completion and automatically scrolls to the next reel.
- **Precision Speed Controls:** Toggle 1.0x, 1.25x, 1.5x, and 2.0x playback rates on demand.
- **Loop Limits:** Replay short clips (2x) before advancing.
- **Sleep Timer:** Automatically stops playback after 15, 30, or 60 minutes.
- **Always-on-Top & Mini Player:** Keep entertaining reels floating above your work or IDE.
- **100% Client-Side Privacy:** Zero tracking, zero analytics, zero data collection.
